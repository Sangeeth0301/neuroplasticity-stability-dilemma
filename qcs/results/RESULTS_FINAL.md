## What is and is not supported

- SUPPORTED: replacing the single-qubit readout with a learned readout over all 10 qubits raises sealed-test MRR from about 0.86 to about 0.97 with the same circuit.
- SUPPORTED: the multi-qubit quantum model matches the parameter-matched linear model on test MRR; it does not beat it, and the MLP is more accurate.
- SUPPORTED: stability is good for every arm (old-task MRR 0.87–1.00); the quantum model shows slightly lower stability and a higher stale-fact error rate than the classical arms.
- SUPPORTED WITH A CAVEAT: after 120 tasks the quantum model adapts to a reversed rule faster than the classical arms. At the training learning rate the gap is large (0.84 vs ~0.2); with each arm's best learning rate and more steps the classical arms partly recover, so the honest statement is 'faster and more reliable', not 'classical arms cannot learn'.
- NOT SUPPORTED: Tr(F) as a plasticity measure — it is noisy and the MLP's did not collapse here.
- NOT TESTED: horizons of 1,000+ tasks, real quantum hardware, a real LLM; only 6 test threads.
